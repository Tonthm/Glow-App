# Animation and Motion

## Purpose

Use this domain for entrance motion, timelines, staggered sequences, scroll motion, hover behavior, and micro-interactions.

## Source-backed references

- [Anime.js](../websites/anime-js.md): motion for entrance, timeline, stagger, scroll, and micro-interaction work; marked as a recommended primary animation engine in the sheet.
- [Aceternity](../websites/aceternity.md): animated hero, card, text, and background components.
- [21st.dev](../websites/21st-dev.md): community motion components and interaction alternatives.

## Selection rule

Choose motion references by the behavior the task needs. The sheet does not require any particular animation library.
