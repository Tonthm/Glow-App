# Particles

## Purpose

Use this domain for particle effects used in backgrounds, heroes, or restrained page accents.

## Source-backed references

- [Casberry Particles](../websites/casberry-particles.md): particle effects for backgrounds, heroes, and accents; the sheet recommends using them where they support content attention.
- [Aceternity](../websites/aceternity.md): simple background and effect references, with a note to avoid distracting from content.

## Selection rule

Use particles only when they support the requested content hierarchy. The sheet does not provide a required effect or configuration.
